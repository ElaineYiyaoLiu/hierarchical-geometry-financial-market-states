## Executable operational inspections
Run `python governance/later_stage_audit.py --mode MODE --record RECORD.json --evidence-root ROOT --output NEW_REPORT.json`.
Modes receipt, capacity, allocation and burn inspect supplied evidence. Empty templates deliberately return BLOCKED. Reports are write-once.

- receipt (Weeks 5/6/8): unique opaque dataset IDs, every frozen scheduled success or failure, supplied registered failure codes and exact reference bytes. sealed_truth_receipt means a permitted custodian receipt document, never sealed truth itself. The auditor hashes referenced manifest/receipt documents; actual observable inventory contents and cryptographic signatures require separate verification. Use the identical approved schedule for generation and processing reconciliation.
- capacity (Week 7): measured seconds per job, integer planned jobs, effective parallelism, full-wave rounding, queue and storage I/O hours, total output storage, complete workload coverage and at least 25% time contingency. Coverage labels include primary, structural_variant, locked_null, registered_geometries, required_subsamples, bootstrap, reconciliation and frozen_evaluation. Labels are a review checklist; they do not prove that counts or durations include all frozen multiplicative work. Provide measured pilot/validation benchmark references and independent review of exact geometry/subsample/bootstrap counts, concurrency, queue assumptions and storage peaks. Current development generator measurements cannot establish Branch B throughput.
- allocation (Week 7): exact one-to-one allocation of all opaque scheduled slots to Batch 1/2. It does not choose allocation or freeze it.
- burn (Week 8): actual permitted operational log references, remaining workload/overhead/time/storage with at least 25% contingency and unchanged frozen science. It does not interpret scientific performance or grant continuation.

All numbers and references in actual records must come from approved/frozen plans and permitted observed operational measurements. No fabricated actual approval, timing or workload is supplied. JSON field conventions are tooling conventions rather than new scientific rules.
