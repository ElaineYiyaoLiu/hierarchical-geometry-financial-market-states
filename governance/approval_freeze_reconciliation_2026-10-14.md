## Reconciliation procedure

1. Obtain the actual decision artifact from its authorized source. Preserve its original text, signature and date.
2. Verify that the named authority and scientific/operational scope match the gate. A general permission to perform repository work does not establish a scientific freeze or Faculty decision.
3. Check exact-byte SHA-256 and the relevant protocol/interface/source version.
4. Locate the matching freeze entry. Compare artifact identity, version, owner, actual approver, time and checksum. Pending placeholder IDs are not completed freezes.
5. For before-inspection requirements, compare against the earliest applicable inspection record. A current decision cannot establish a historical freeze.
6. Keep restricted evidence private. Supply a permitted public attestation only where the project contract allows it.
7. Update the register only after the evidence review. Supply approval_ref, approval_sha256 and freeze_id from actual records; preserve scoped statuses when scope remains limited.
8. Re-run the gate audit and retain its output. Its REVIEWABLE result is not an automatic approval.
